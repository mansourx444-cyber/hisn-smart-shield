🔗How does HISN detect suspicious links?

Every link that arrives by SMS or WhatsApp automatically passes 13 checks before the user opens it; the results are combined into one risk score with the reason.

Extract the link from the text→Parse the domain→13 checks→Combine with the message text analysis→Risk score + reason→First / second warning
